How it works

Meet someone. They tap. They have you.

Each sticker holds a tiny NFC chip, the same technology as tap-to-pay. We program it with the address of your personal page, so any modern iPhone or Android phone opens it in a second.

  1. Tap

    The other person holds their phone near your sticker: on the back of your phone, your desk or your counter.

  2. Your page opens

    Your personal page appears on their screen. Nothing to download, nothing to type.

  3. They save you

    One button adds your name, number and email to their contacts. They call, text or book you whenever they are ready.

Where people put their 5 stickers

  • Back of the phone
  • Desk or reception
  • Shop counter
  • Laptop or tablet
  • Car or work van
  • Front door

Your page always has the same address. When something changes (a new number, a new service, a new photo), we update the page and every sticker shows the new version. Nothing to reprint.
